Registration Fellowship

VDAT 2026 invites applications for registration fellowship support from eligible students, research scholars, postdoctoral researchers, and faculty members working in VLSI Design, Semiconductor Technology, Electronic Design Automation (EDA), Hardware Security, Advanced Packaging, Quantum Computing, and related areas.
